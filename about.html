Gary J. Hobbins

His story

From the stage to the Self.

Gary Hobbins set out on an adventure to become a professional comedian. He loved himself and others. He unintentionally created a universal blueprint that can be referenced for enlightenment.

He eliminated confusing religious doctrines from his thinking. He became honest with himself. He surrendered, praying only for strength. He accepted his body, his appearance, his family, and the conditions of his life. He developed the foundation of an honest conscience.

He reasoned that being honest with himself and others gave him the confidence to perform. He developed authenticity. He realized that honesty connects us, while dishonesty separates us. Complete honesty merged him with the psychology of his audiences, which he understood as Universal Consciousness.

The journey

One of the rarest compound spiritual experiences in history.

It was an awakening to Universal Consciousness. It was a union. It was the yoking of the lower self to our shared Universal Self. It was a communion with the Hindu notion of the Atman and the Mosaic image of God. It was yoga.
