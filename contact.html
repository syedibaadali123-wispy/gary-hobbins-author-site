Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this a religious book?

No. This is not a religious book. Jesus is not discussed as God. Gary explains that the axioms of Jesus resemble Zen Buddhist koans and carry the spirit of enlightenment.

Who is the book for?

It is for everyone — a profound story of our potential for personal merging into the clarity of interpersonal psychology.

Where can I buy the book?

The Mind’s New Liberation is available on Amazon. You can also use the “Buy the Book” button on any page.

Can schools use the Lesson Plan Directive?

Yes. The book contains a Lesson Plan Directive for Home and School dealing with the early formation of honest consciences. Choose “School / Lesson Plan Directive” in the form to get in touch.
