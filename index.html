A universal blueprint

Complete honesty transforms the lower self into the pure Higher Self.

Setting out to become a professional comedian, Gary unintentionally created a universal blueprint that can be referenced for enlightenment.

i.

Honesty

Being honest with himself and others gave him the confidence to perform. The purity of honesty, as opposed to dishonesty, leads to knowing.

ii.

Acceptance

He surrendered, praying only for strength. He accepted his body, his appearance, his family, and the conditions of his life.

iii.

Union

Complete honesty merged him with the psychology of his audiences, which he understood as Universal Consciousness.

The vision

We are the same inside.

An explosion of golden liquid firelight surrounded his body. A vision of people from different races appeared in a cloud. He heard the Zen Buddhist koan “WORDS ARE TRAPS.” He realized that we are the same inside.
